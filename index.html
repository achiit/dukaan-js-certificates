Frequently asked questions

Important things to know before requesting a voucher.

Are these official certification exam vouchers?

Voucher source and authorization must be confirmed for each offer. Ask for written proof of authorized supply, issuer terms and redemption instructions before making payment. This website does not claim affiliation with AWS, Microsoft, Google, Cisco, CompTIA or PeopleCert.

Why are prices not displayed?

Voucher prices and availability can vary by exam, region, eligibility and expiry. Request a quote with your exact exam code so the details can be checked.

Can I use a voucher in any country?

Not always. Some vouchers have country, region, language, candidate or expiry restrictions. Confirm these details before purchase.

How do I request a quote?

Complete the enquiry form below. It will open WhatsApp with your details ready to send, once you add your business WhatsApp number to the website settings.
